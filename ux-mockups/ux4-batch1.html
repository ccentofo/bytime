How overtime is worked out

Overtime is hours worked over 40 in your workweek (starts Monday), for non-exempt employees only. Leave doesn't count toward the 40. Weeks that cross the pay-period edge use the days on both sides.

  • Defaults to the last completed pay period, not "this month", with presets for this period and the one before.

  • A totals row and right-aligned numbers; exempt employees show "—" for overtime instead of 0.
