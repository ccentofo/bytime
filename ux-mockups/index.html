What changed and why

  • The grid is the page. The four stat cards and Recent periods table are gone. Status is one line, and past periods live under "Past timesheets". The first row of hours now sits about 260px from the top instead of about 1,000px.
  • One status, one period control. "Draft" appears once (it was shown three times). Arrows plus "Choose period" replace three separate navigators.
  • Each day header shows how full the day is. A thin bar fills toward 8 hours, so the shape of the period is visible at a glance without "LATE" badges on every past day.
  • Empty is blank. No "—" in every cell. Weekends are shaded, future days are lighter, and today has a blue top edge.
  • Row labels are one line plus the code. Rows are 44px instead of about 85px. Leave rows are named by type ("Annual leave", "Holiday"), not all "Leave".
  • Built for keyboard and screen readers (508). Every cell is a real control: arrows move, typing enters hours, N adds a note. Notes are reachable without a mouse. Submit says why it's unavailable instead of just being grey.

The brand blue #4F6BFF stays: it's still the focus ring, today marker, progress bars and logo dot, where 3:1 is the requirement. Only filled buttons and blue text move one step darker to #3F55E6. Toggle dark mode at the top right to see both themes.

Decision UX-D1

  • Default: filled buttons and blue text use #3F55E6; #4F6BFF remains the accent. If you'd rather keep the brighter blue on buttons, the only compliant alternative is dark text on the blue, which looks heavier.
  • Green stops being a button color. Submit is a primary action like any other; green is kept for "approved" and "active" status only.

How the groups behave

  • The group containing the current page is open; the others stay closed, so the menu is short by default. Your open/closed choice is remembered.
  • Groups are buttons that announce "expanded" or "collapsed" to screen readers; the current page is marked for assistive technology, not just highlighted.
